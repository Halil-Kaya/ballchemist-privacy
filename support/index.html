Common questions

How do I play? Drag to aim, let go to shoot. Your balls bounce off the walls and break numbered bricks. If a brick reaches the line, you lose balls equal to its number.

How do fusions work? Some balls combine into new ones. Your first fusion appears on level 11; discovered fusions are listed in the Codex.

I lost a level. What now? Try again any time. You can also watch an optional video to continue.

I bought "Remove ads" but still see ads. Open Settings and tap Restore purchases, using the same Apple ID or Google account you bought with.

How do I change my ad choices? Open Settings and tap Privacy choices (where it applies).
