Ballchemist – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Ballchemist: Brick Breaker ("the game") is made by Halil Kaya ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game uses and why. The game has no accounts, no sign-in and no chat, and we do not sell your personal information.

Information stored on your device

Your progress (levels, coins, upgrades, discovered balls and settings), whether you bought "Remove ads", and your consent choices are saved only on your device. We do not receive them. Deleting the game deletes this data.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads through Google AdMob: optional rewarded videos (for example to continue a lost level or double your coins) and an occasional full-screen ad after you win a level. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect device information such as the device model and operating system, an approximate location derived from your IP address, ad interactions, and, only if you allow it, your device's advertising identifier. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time with Privacy choices in the game's settings. Learn more in how Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

In-app purchase

You can buy "Remove ads" once. The payment is handled entirely by the Apple App Store or Google Play; we never see your payment details. We only learn, on your device, that the purchase exists so the game can turn ads off and restore the purchase.

Gameplay analytics and crash reports (Google Firebase)

To see where players get stuck and to fix bugs, the game sends anonymous gameplay events to Google Firebase Analytics: for example when a level starts and ends, how many shots it took, tutorial progress, which balls were unlocked, and whether an ad was offered or watched. Firebase also records an app-instance identifier, the device model, operating system, app version and an approximate country. If the game crashes, Firebase Crashlytics sends a crash report with the device state and the last gameplay events. This data is not used to identify you. See Firebase's privacy information.

What we do not do

  • We do not ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • We do not sell personal information.
  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The game is not directed at children.

Your choices

  • Change ad consent in the game under Settings → Privacy choices (where it applies).
  • On iPhone, limit ad tracking under Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • On Android, reset or delete your advertising ID under Settings → Privacy → Ads (the exact path depends on your device).
  • Delete the game to remove all data stored on your device.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
